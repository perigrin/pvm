## What I checked by running it, at e3432c0f

I ran experiments as `go test -overlay` files kept in my scratch directory. The repository is untouched.

- **The measured claims hold.** d0ba says `print $fh [];`, `$a <=> $b <=> $c;` and `1 .. 2 .. 3;` are each recognized and then refused by `Parse` as `precedence` at byte 0; they are. I also confirmed these refusals:
  - `1 plus 2;\nprint <<EOT;\nno terminator\n` is lex-unmapped at byte 16 (d166);
  - `1 .. 2 .. 3;\nprint <<EOT;…` is lex-unmapped at byte 19 (3262);
  - `foo plus 2 plus 3;` is scope at byte 4;
  - `1 plus 2;\n` is scope at byte 2.
- **The switch-over does not reach the port tests unexpectedly.** I instrumented `recognizeSource`, which has 136 call sites that 3262 does not enumerate. Across the whole `internal/chart` package, the only inputs that recognition accepts and V1 refuses by precedence are the three in `TestPrecedenceIgnoredByRecognition`, which 3262 retires. Of the 760 port snippets, none is refused by precedence alone.
- **The zero-width `;` rule in d0e1 is safe** (F8-P1). For all 1,296 kept referee sentences I compared two forests:
  - narrowed lattice, then the no-precedence recognizer, then Build;
  - V1's referee path: Build over the full lattice, then `narrow`.

  The forests are identical node for node, and neither has a reachable zero-width `;` leaf. So narrowing the lattice before recognition (d247) adds nothing that d0e1's rule would have to remove, and the rule cannot hide a difference.
- **Run time is no risk.** With `T1_CORPUS` set, the ratchet package takes 36 s and `TestPrecedenceCorpusMatchesReference` takes 3.8 s, so the added corpus differentials will not reach `go test`'s 10-minute default.
- **CI has perl.git.** The "Ratchets (corpus-backed)" job sets `PERL5_CORPUS` and runs `make chart-precedence-reference` under pipefail, so d10d's claim that CI finds a perl.git-only difference is true.
- **The answer-key count is right.** `baseline.txt` holds exactly 27 keys once you drop `speed-`, `lex-overhead`, `alloc-`, `gc-`, `items-per-token` and `set-reuse`, which matches d2d1's filter. `precedence-covered` is computed from the fixtures, not from `Constrain`, so retiring the pass cannot move it.
- **Every criterion starts red.** I checked each one: grep-shaped criteria are red before their work, and test-name criteria are red because the test does not exist yet.
