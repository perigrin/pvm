# chain-review chart-perly-w4, round 1: review-alignment (coverage lens)

**Recommendation: modify.** The coverage itself is complete. All 66 residual rows and the F2-11 crumb are mapped, every design requirement has an issue, and no issue is scope creep. But four blocking defects sit in what the issues assert. Two would trip a §7 stop on work that is correct. One breaks perigrin's stage order. One breaks the rule that every criterion is red before its work.

## Coverage, in numbers

These come from the live chain (`git zhi milestone show` / `issue list --format json`) at tree `e3432c0f`:

- **Scope creep.** I found none. Every issue and every new test traces to a design section, a ruling or a residual row.
- **Model labels.** 17 of 17 issues carry one.
- **Criteria keyed to issue IDs.** None are. Criteria key on `e3432c0f` or `e3432c0f..HEAD`.
- **Criteria green before their work.** I ran all 179 paren-wrapped criteria (not 162) against the current tree. 177 are red. 2 are green before the work (AL4).
